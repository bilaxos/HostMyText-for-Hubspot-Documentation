User guide for reps

Audience: reps who send texts from HubSpot.

Read 06 Quick start first.

The Send SMS card

Section titled “The Send SMS card”

The card is the Send SMS tab on a contact record.

The card has these parts:

Part Use
From Your HostMyText number. The card opens on a connected number.
To The phone number to text.
Message The text.
File upload One picture to send with the text.
Send Sends the text.
Download SMS history Prepares a CSV file of the SMS history.

The From list

Section titled “The From list”

The list shows your numbers.

A number that is not connected to HubSpot shows the words “(not connected to HubSpot)”.

You cannot send from that number.

Ask your admin to connect it.

The To field

Section titled “The To field”

The card fills To with the phone number of the contact.

If the contact has no phone number, the card uses the mobile phone number.

You can change the number.

If you type a different number, the card shows this warning: “This number doesn’t match the phone number on this contact.”

You can still send.

Send a text

Section titled “Send a text”
  1. Open the Send SMS tab.
  2. Choose From.
  3. Check To.
  4. Type the text in Message.
  5. Click Send.

Send stays off if From, To or Message is empty.

HubSpot shows “Message sent.” after the send.

The text appears in the conversation of the contact.

Send a picture

Section titled “Send a picture”
  1. Fill From, To and Message. On the card, a picture needs a text.
  2. In the file upload, choose one picture.
  3. Click Send.

The picture must be a PNG or a JPEG file.

You can attach one picture.

HubSpot shows “Message sent.” when the text is sent.

Open the conversation to check that the picture is in the text.

Reply in the HubSpot inbox

Section titled “Reply in the HubSpot inbox”

The inbox shows the texts of your contacts.

  1. Open the conversation.
  2. Type your reply.
  3. Send the reply.

HostMyText sends the reply as a text.

You can attach a picture to a reply.

The reply is sent with your HostMyText login.

You must have linked your seat. See 06 Quick start.

You must be able to use the number in HostMyText.

HubSpot marks the reply as FAILED if HostMyText rejects it.

The reason shows with the message. The reasons are in 05 Troubleshooting and FAQ.

You cannot start a new conversation in the inbox.

To start a new conversation, use the Send SMS tab on the contact.

Receive texts and pictures

Section titled “Receive texts and pictures”

A text from a contact appears in the inbox.

A text from a contact also appears on the contact record.

A picture in a text appears in the conversation.

If the number of the sender is not in HubSpot, the app creates a contact.

The first name of the new contact is the phone number.

Message status

Section titled “Message status”

HubSpot shows a sent message as sent.

HubSpot has no “Delivered” status for this app.

If the carrier reports a failure, HubSpot changes the message to FAILED.

The change takes about 30 seconds.

Opt-outs

Section titled “Opt-outs”

A contact can send STOP to opt out.

A contact can send START to opt in again.

When a contact opted out:

  • The card shows “This contact has opted out of receiving messages and cannot be sent to.”
  • Send stays off.
  • A reply in the inbox turns to FAILED.
  • A workflow step for this contact fails.

HostMyText checks the opt-out before each text.

The contact property “HostMyText Opted Out” shows the state.

If you change the property in HubSpot, the opt-out does not change.

To change an opt-out, use HostMyText.

Download SMS history

Section titled “Download SMS history”
  1. Open the contact.
  2. Open the Send SMS tab.
  3. Click Download SMS history.
  4. When the button changes, click Download SMS history (CSV).

The file has these columns: Date, Direction, From, Message.

The file contains SMS messages only.

The file shows the messages as HubSpot shows them.

It can differ from the history in HostMyText.

A very long history can be cut off.

If the card shows “Could not prepare download — try again”, click the button again.

Texts from workflows

Section titled “Texts from workflows”

A workflow can send texts to your contacts.

These texts appear in the conversation of the contact.

HostMyText sends them with the login of the automation sender.

What does not appear in HubSpot

Section titled “What does not appear in HubSpot”
  • Bulk campaigns.
  • Group messages.
  • Messages from before your admin connected the app.
