Use-case catalogue

Audience: admins, reps and the dev team.

Each use case shows who does each step and what the system does.

The diagrams name no internal host and no port.

Names used

Section titled “Names used”
Name Meaning
Rep A HubSpot user who texts.
Admin A HubSpot administrator who is also a HostMyText organization admin.
Contact The person who gets and sends texts.
HubSpot The HubSpot account of the customer.
HostMyText HostMyText, with the carrier.

Use case 1: A rep texts a known contact

Section titled “Use case 1: A rep texts a known contact”

Goal

Section titled “Goal”

A rep sends a text to a contact. The contact replies. The rep answers in the inbox.

Before you start

Section titled “Before you start”
  • The app is installed.
  • The number is connected.
  • The rep linked a seat.
  • The contact has a phone number.

Steps

Section titled “Steps”
Step Who Action What happens
1 Rep Opens the contact and the Send SMS tab. The card shows the numbers of the rep. It fills To with the contact’s phone number.
2 Rep Chooses From, types the Message, clicks Send. HostMyText checks the opt-out status and sends the text. HubSpot shows “Message sent.”
3 HostMyText Sends the text to the carrier. The text appears in the conversation of the contact. This can take a short time.
4 Contact Replies. The reply appears in the HubSpot inbox and on the contact record.
5 Rep Opens the conversation, types a reply, sends it. HostMyText sends the reply as a real text. 
sequenceDiagram
    actor Rep
    participant HS as HubSpot
    participant HMT as HostMyText
    actor Contact

    Rep->>HS: Send SMS tab: From, To, Message, Send
    HS->>HMT: send request
    HMT->>HMT: check opt-out
    HMT->>Contact: text
    HMT->>HS: show the text in the conversation
    Contact->>HMT: reply
    HMT->>HS: show the reply in the inbox
    Rep->>HS: type a reply in the inbox
    HS->>HMT: reply request
    HMT->>Contact: text

Other paths

Section titled “Other paths”
Case Result
The contact opted out. The card shows that the contact opted out. Send is off. A reply in the inbox turns to FAILED.
The number is not connected. The card warns and Send is off. Ask an admin.
The rep attaches a picture. The text goes as MMS. The card needs a message text with the picture. The card shows “Message sent.” even when the picture fails.
The carrier reports a failure. HubSpot changes the message to FAILED after about 30 seconds.
The rep has not linked a seat. The card shows “Link your HostMyText account”. A reply in the inbox turns to FAILED.

Use case 2: A new number texts in

Section titled “Use case 2: A new number texts in”

Goal

Section titled “Goal”

A person that HubSpot does not know sends a text. The rep sees the text and can answer.

Steps

Section titled “Steps”
Step Who Action What happens
1 Contact Sends a text to a connected number. HostMyText receives the text.
2 HostMyText Looks for a contact with this phone number in HubSpot. HostMyText searches the phone and mobile phone properties.
3 HostMyText Finds no contact. HostMyText creates a contact. The first name is the phone number. “HostMyText Auto Created” is Yes.
4 HostMyText Waits until HubSpot can find the new contact. This can take about 25 seconds. The limit is about 36 seconds.
5 HostMyText Shows the text in the HubSpot inbox. The conversation carries the phone number as its name.
6 Rep Opens the conversation and replies. HostMyText sends the reply. 
sequenceDiagram
    actor Contact
    participant HMT as HostMyText
    participant HS as HubSpot
    actor Rep

    Contact->>HMT: text from an unknown number
    HMT->>HS: search for a contact by phone
    HS-->>HMT: no contact
    HMT->>HS: create contact (name = phone number)
    HMT->>HS: wait until the contact is found
    HMT->>HS: show the text in the inbox
    Rep->>HS: open the conversation, reply
    HS->>HMT: reply request
    HMT->>Contact: text

Other paths

Section titled “Other paths”
Case Result
A contact with this number exists. HostMyText puts the text on this contact. No new contact.
The stored number has a different format. HostMyText also searches the two calculated phone properties. It finds the contact.
Two contacts have this number. HubSpot can show a visitor. Merge or remove the duplicate.
Many new numbers text at the same time. A conversation can show “Unknown Visitor”. An admin fixes it by hand. See 05.
The text has a picture. The picture shows in the inbox. If HostMyText cannot reach the file, the text shows without it.
The text goes to a group. Group messages do not appear in HubSpot.

Use case 3: A contact sends STOP, then START

Section titled “Use case 3: A contact sends STOP, then START”

Goal

Section titled “Goal”

An opt-out blocks sends. An opt-in allows them again.

Steps

Section titled “Steps”
Step Who Action What happens
1 Contact Sends STOP. HostMyText records the opt-out.
2 HostMyText Updates the contact in HubSpot. “HostMyText Opted Out” is Yes. “HostMyText Permission Source” shows “Inbound STOP”. This takes seconds.
3 Rep Opens the Send SMS tab. The card shows that the contact opted out. Send is off.
4 Rep Replies in the inbox. The reply turns to FAILED, with the opt-out text.
5 HubSpot A workflow step runs for the contact. The step fails with the opt-out text. The workflow continues.
6 Contact Sends START. HostMyText removes the opt-out. HostMyText sends its confirmation text. The confirmation also shows in HubSpot.
7 HostMyText Updates the contact. “HostMyText Opted Out” is No.
8 Rep Sends a text. The text goes out. 
sequenceDiagram
    actor Contact
    participant HMT as HostMyText
    participant HS as HubSpot
    actor Rep

    Contact->>HMT: STOP
    HMT->>HMT: record opt-out
    HMT->>HS: update contact properties
    Rep->>HS: try to send
    HS-->>Rep: blocked, contact opted out
    Contact->>HMT: START
    HMT->>HMT: remove opt-out
    HMT->>Contact: confirmation text
    HMT->>HS: update contact properties
    Rep->>HS: send
    HS->>HMT: send request
    HMT->>Contact: text

Other paths

Section titled “Other paths”
Case Result
The contact opted out on another connected number. The property stays Yes after START on this number. HostMyText decides the final state.
The contact sends STOP to a number that is not connected. HubSpot does not change.
A rep changes “HostMyText Opted Out” in HubSpot. Nothing changes. Use HostMyText.
The card cannot check the consent. It warns. HostMyText checks again before it sends.

Use case 4: A workflow sends a welcome text

Section titled “Use case 4: A workflow sends a welcome text”

Goal

Section titled “Goal”

A contact submits a form. A workflow sends a text with the contact’s first name.

Before you start

Section titled “Before you start”
  • The number is connected.
  • An admin chose an automation sender.
  • The automation sender can use the number.

Steps

Section titled “Steps”
Step Who Action What happens
1 Admin Builds a contact workflow. The trigger is a form submission. The action is Send HostMyText message. The action has the fields Message and Send from.
2 Admin Types Hi {{firstname}}, thank you for your request. Chooses a number. Turns the workflow on. The list of numbers shows only connected numbers that the sender can use.
3 Contact Submits the form. HubSpot enrolls the contact.
4 HubSpot Runs the action. HostMyText takes the mobile phone number, or the phone number.
5 HostMyText Checks the sender, the number access and the opt-out status. The text goes out as the automation sender.
6 HubSpot Shows the result. The step shows “Message sent.” The text appears in the conversation of the contact. 
sequenceDiagram
    actor Contact
    participant HS as HubSpot
    participant HMT as HostMyText

    Contact->>HS: submits a form
    HS->>HS: enroll the contact in the workflow
    HS->>HMT: run the action (message, number, contact data)
    HMT->>HMT: check automation sender, number access, opt-out
    HMT->>Contact: text
    HMT-->>HS: result Message sent
    HMT->>HS: show the text in the conversation

Other paths

Section titled “Other paths”
Case Result
No automation sender. The step fails: “No automation sender is configured for this portal; pick one in the app’s settings.”
The sender cannot use the number. The step fails: “The automation sender does not have access to the From number.” Settings shows a warning.
The number in the action is not connected to HubSpot. The step fails: “The From number is not connected to HubSpot.” No text is sent. This can happen after a number is disconnected, or after the app is uninstalled and installed again.
The field Send from is empty. The step fails: “No sending number was configured on this action.”
The contact has no phone number. The step fails: “This contact has no phone number on file.”
The contact opted out. The step fails with the opt-out text.
A text must go later. Add the HubSpot Delay step before the action.
HubSpot sends the same call twice. One text goes out.

A failed step does not stop the workflow.
