Troubleshooting and FAQ

Audience: HubSpot administrators, HostMyText organization administrators, and reps.

Each entry starts with the message that the app shows.

If a fix does not work, contact HostMyText support: https://www.hostmy.com/get-support

Install problems

Section titled “Install problems”

The install messages are in 02 Install and connect, section “If something goes wrong”.

Card problems

Section titled “Card problems”

The card is the Send SMS tab on the contact record.

Could not load your phone numbers. The card could not read your numbers. Click Retry. If it continues, contact support.
This number isn’t connected to HubSpot, so you can’t send from it here. Connect it on the app’s Settings page first. The number in From is not connected. Choose a connected number. Or ask an admin to connect the number.
This contact has opted out of receiving messages and cannot be sent to. The contact sent STOP. You cannot send to this contact. The contact must send START.
Couldn’t verify this contact’s consent status. The send will still be checked before it goes out. The card could not check the opt-out now. You can still send. HostMyText checks again before it sends.
This number doesn’t match the phone number on this contact. The number in To is not the contact’s phone number or mobile phone number. Check the number. You can still send to a different number.
Message failed to send. The send was rejected. Read any message under the form. Try again. If it continues, contact support.
Could not prepare download — try again The history file did not start. Click the button again.

The From list shows the numbers that you can use in HostMyText.

The card opens on a connected number.

The Send button stays off while a number that is not connected is chosen.

Message problems

Section titled “Message problems”

A reply from the inbox turned to FAILED

Section titled “A reply from the inbox turned to FAILED”

HubSpot shows the message as FAILED when HostMyText rejects the reply.

The failure reason shows with the message.

HubSpot can take about 30 seconds to show FAILED.

Reason What to do
This contact has opted out of receiving messages and cannot be sent to. You cannot send to this contact.
No HostMyText user is linked to this HubSpot seat. Link your seat. See step B in 03.
The linked user does not have access to the From number. Ask for access to the number in HostMyText.
The From number is not connected to HubSpot. Ask an admin to connect the number.
Carrier reported delivery failure (status id N). The carrier did not deliver the text. Check the contact’s number.

A received text has no picture

Section titled “A received text has no picture”

The text shows in HubSpot without the picture.

Contact HostMyText support.

The conversation shows the label “Unknown Visitor”

Section titled “The conversation shows the label “Unknown Visitor””

HubSpot labels a conversation “Unknown Visitor” when it cannot match the number to a contact at the time of the first message.

The app does not fix this label by itself.

HubSpot has no automatic way to change it.

You can fix it by hand in HubSpot:

  1. Open the conversation in the inbox.
  2. Add the visitor as a contact.
  3. Enter the contact details.
  4. Enter the phone number in Phone number or Mobile phone number.
  5. Save.

The label changes to the name of the contact.

HubSpot can also let you choose an existing contact instead of a new contact.

Two contacts have the same phone number

Section titled “Two contacts have the same phone number”

HubSpot can show a visitor instead of a contact when two contacts share a phone number.

Merge or remove the duplicate contacts in HubSpot.

A contact got two replies

Section titled “A contact got two replies”

A HostMyText auto-reply and a HubSpot workflow both answered.

Turn off the HostMyText auto-replies on the number.

A contact was created that nobody made

Section titled “A contact was created that nobody made”

When an unknown number sends a text, the app creates a contact.

The contact has the property “HostMyText Auto Created” set to Yes.

The first name of the contact is the phone number.

You can edit the contact in HubSpot.

History download problems

Section titled “History download problems”

The file does not contain all messages

Section titled “The file does not contain all messages”

The file contains only SMS messages.

The file shows the messages as HubSpot shows them.

It does not contain bulk messages, group messages, or messages from before you connected the app.

A very long history can be cut off.

Archived conversations are not in the file.

The file can differ from the history in HostMyText.

FAQ

Section titled “FAQ”

Can I start a conversation from the HubSpot inbox? No. Start from the card on the contact record. You can reply in the inbox.

Why does HubSpot not show “Delivered”? HubSpot has no Delivered status for this app. HubSpot changes a message to FAILED if the carrier reports a failure.

Do bulk campaigns show in HubSpot? No.

Do group messages show in HubSpot? No.

Are old messages imported? No. Messages from before you connected the app do not appear.

Can I send more than one picture? No. The card sends one picture. It must be a PNG or a JPEG file.

I changed “HostMyText Opted Out” in HubSpot. Why did nothing change? HubSpot properties do not change the opt-out. Use HostMyText.

Which phone number does a workflow use? The mobile phone number of the contact. If it is empty, the phone number.

Why did HubSpot show a warning when I installed the app? HubSpot has not reviewed the app yet. See 02.

What happens to my messages when the app is uninstalled? The messages that are already in HubSpot stay in HubSpot. HostMyText keeps all of its messages, and texting in HostMyText does not change. See 04.

I installed the app again. Why do my numbers show “Not connected”? An uninstall deletes the connections of the numbers in HubSpot. Connect each number again on the settings page. See 04.

Can I use the app outside the United States? The app is for use in the United States only.
