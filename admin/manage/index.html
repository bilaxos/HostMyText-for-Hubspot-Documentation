Admin guide

Audience: HubSpot administrators and HostMyText organization administrators.

This guide covers the tasks you do after setup.

First finish 03 Setup guide.

To open the app settings, use this path: Marketplace icon in the top bar, then Connected apps, then HostMyText in the My Apps list, then Settings.

Connect and disconnect numbers

Section titled “Connect and disconnect numbers”

The section Phone numbers lists every number of your HostMyText organization.

Each number shows one status:

  • Connected: texts of this number go to HubSpot.
  • Not connected: texts of this number do not go to HubSpot.

Who can change a number

Section titled “Who can change a number”

Only a HostMyText organization admin can connect or disconnect a number.

You can change only the numbers that you can use in HostMyText.

For other numbers, the page shows “No access to this number in HostMyText”.

A user who is not an admin sees the list but cannot change it.

Connect a number

Section titled “Connect a number”
  1. Turn off the HostMyText auto-replies on the number.
  2. Check the inbox in HubSpot inbox for new connections.
  3. Click Connect next to the number.

If you skip step 1, a contact can get two replies.

One reply comes from the HostMyText auto-reply.

One reply comes from a HubSpot workflow.

HostMyText does not turn off auto-replies for a connected number.

Disconnect a number

Section titled “Disconnect a number”
  1. Click Disconnect next to the number.
  2. Click Confirm disconnect.

You can click Cancel instead.

After you disconnect a number:

  • New texts of this number do not go to HubSpot.
  • A reply that a rep types in HubSpot for this number does not send.
  • HubSpot does not correct the status of a message that fails just after the disconnect.
  • The conversations and messages that are already in HubSpot stay in HubSpot.

You can connect the number again later.

The connect step uses the same HubSpot connection when it already exists.

Disconnect HostMyText from the HubSpot account

Section titled “Disconnect HostMyText from the HubSpot account”

Use this when you stop using the app in this HubSpot account.

This disconnects the whole HubSpot account. It is not the same as the disconnect of one number.

Who can disconnect the account

Section titled “Who can disconnect the account”

Only a HostMyText organization admin can disconnect the account.

Other users do not see the section Disconnect HubSpot.

Disconnect the account

Section titled “Disconnect the account”
  1. Open the app settings.
  2. Scroll to the section Disconnect HubSpot.
  3. Click Disconnect HubSpot account.
  4. Read the warning. Click Confirm disconnect.

You can click Cancel instead.

After a few seconds the page shows “HostMyText was disconnected”.

What happens when you disconnect the account

Section titled “What happens when you disconnect the account”
  • HubSpot uninstalls the app from the account.
  • HubSpot sends an email about the uninstall to the Super Admins of the account.
  • The HostMyText card and the app settings page are gone.
  • Every number shows as not connected. New texts do not go to HubSpot.
  • The conversations and messages that are already in HubSpot stay in HubSpot.
  • A HubSpot workflow that uses the HostMyText action stays in the workflow list. The action card shows “Changes needed”.
  • The five HostMyText contact properties stay in HubSpot. They are not deleted. HostMyText does not update them any more.
  • Texting in HostMyText does not change. Your numbers, contacts and messages in HostMyText stay as they are.

To use the app again, install it again. See 02 Install and connect.

Then connect your numbers again.

A workflow after you install the app again

Section titled “A workflow after you install the app again”

A workflow that used the HostMyText action keeps its saved values.

The field Send from lists only connected numbers. Until you connect the number again, the field is disabled. HubSpot shows this red note under it: “Because of limitations with the third party app, we can’t check if this value still exists. Try searching for this value again, or ignore this warning if you believe it’s incorrect.”

Connect the number again on the app settings page. Then open the workflow action again. The red note is gone and you can choose a number.

The workflow runs and sends the text. The step shows “Message sent.”

A workflow can send only from a connected number. If the workflow has a number in Send from that is not connected, the step fails with “The From number is not connected to HubSpot.” and no text is sent. Connect the number, or choose a connected number in Send from.

Messages stay in HubSpot after a disconnect. The field Send from is the one place where an old workflow needs your attention.

If someone uninstalls the app in HubSpot

Section titled “If someone uninstalls the app in HubSpot”

A HubSpot admin can also uninstall the app in HubSpot. There are two places:

  • Marketplace, then Connected apps: click Actions at the right side of the HostMyText row, then Uninstall.
  • The HostMyText app page: click Actions at the right side of the page, then Uninstall HostMyText.

A user who is not allowed to uninstall apps sees the button as disabled.

The result is the same as after the button above.

HostMyText notices the uninstall when the next text of a connected number arrives or is sent. Then HostMyText clears the numbers on its side.

HostMyText also clears them when you install the app again.

After you install the app again, the numbers show as Not connected. Connect them again. See the section “Connect a number”.

Change the automation sender

Section titled “Change the automation sender”

The automation sender is the linked user that sends workflow texts.

The sender can use only the numbers that this user can use in HostMyText.

Change the sender

Section titled “Change the sender”
  1. Open the section Automation sender.
  2. Choose another user.
  3. Click Save.

Only one user is the automation sender at a time.

Remove the sender

Section titled “Remove the sender”
  1. Choose No automation sender.
  2. Click Save.

Workflow steps fail when no sender is set.

Read the warning

Section titled “Read the warning”

The page can show this warning: “This sender can’t send from: (numbers) — workflow sends using these numbers will fail until access is granted.”

The workflow step fails for the listed numbers.

Choose another sender.

You can also give this user access to the numbers in HostMyText.

Opt-outs and the HubSpot properties

Section titled “Opt-outs and the HubSpot properties”

HostMyText is the source of truth for opt-outs.

HostMyText checks the opt-out status again before each text.

The app copies the opt-out state into HubSpot contact properties.

HubSpot shows the properties in the group Contact information.

The properties

Section titled “The properties”
Property label What it shows
HostMyText Opted Out Yes when the contact opted out. No when the contact did not.
HostMyText Permission Level Conversational, Marketing, or None.
HostMyText Permission Source Where the permission state came from. For an opt-out, the value is “Inbound STOP” or “Manual”.
HostMyText Permission Last Synced The time of the last update from HostMyText.
HostMyText Auto Created Yes when the app created the contact from a text of an unknown number.

You can use these properties in lists and in workflow filters.

Several numbers

Section titled “Several numbers”

The app looks at all connected numbers of your organization.

“HostMyText Opted Out” is Yes when the contact opted out on any connected number.

“HostMyText Permission Level” shows the lowest level of all connected numbers.

If one connected number has no permission record for the contact, the level shows None.

“HostMyText Permission Source” names the numbers behind an opt-out.

A change on a number that is not connected does not change the contact in HubSpot.

Changes in HubSpot do nothing

Section titled “Changes in HubSpot do nothing”

HubSpot lets you edit these properties.

An edit does not change the opt-out in HostMyText.

An edit does not unblock a send.

To change an opt-out, use HostMyText.

When a contact sends STOP or START

Section titled “When a contact sends STOP or START”

STOP and START are handled in HostMyText.

The properties change within seconds.

A blocked send shows this message in HubSpot: “This contact has opted out of receiving messages and cannot be sent to.”

A contact that opted out on one number can stay opted out in HubSpot after START on another number.

HostMyText decides the final state.
