Setup guide

Audience: HubSpot administrators and HostMyText organization administrators.

Finish 02 Install and connect first.

Setup order

Section titled “Setup order”
Step Who Task
A Administrator Add the Send SMS card to the contact view.
B Administrator Link your seat.
C Administrator Connect your numbers.
D Administrator Choose the automation sender.
E Each rep Link your seat.
F Administrator Build a workflow that sends a text.

Do the steps in this order.

You need a linked seat before you can open the number settings.

You need a linked seat before you can choose an automation sender.

Step A: Add the Send SMS card to the contact view

Section titled “Step A: Add the Send SMS card to the contact view”

The Send SMS card is not on the contact record by default.

You add it once.

There are two ways.

Way 1: From the Contacts page

Section titled “Way 1: From the Contacts page”
  1. Open the Contacts page in HubSpot.
  2. Click Customize.
  3. Click Default View.
  4. Click Add card.
  5. Click Card Library.
  6. Search for Send SMS.
  7. Add the card.
  8. Move the card to the place you want.

Way 2: From the app settings

Section titled “Way 2: From the app settings”
  1. Open the app settings of HostMyText.
  2. Click App Cards.
  3. Find Send SMS.
  4. Click Manage Locations.
  5. Add the card where you want it.

The card shows as a tab named Send SMS on the contact record.

Open the app settings

Section titled “Open the app settings”

You use the app settings in steps C and D.

  1. Click the Marketplace icon in the top bar.
  2. Click Connected apps.
  3. In the My Apps list, click HostMyText.
  4. Click Settings.

Step B: Link your seat

Section titled “Step B: Link your seat”

A seat is one HubSpot user.

Linking connects your HubSpot user to your HostMyText login.

You do this one time for each user.

  1. Open a contact in HubSpot.
  2. Open the Send SMS tab.
  3. A window opens the first time. It says “Link your HostMyText account”.
  4. If no window opens, click Link your HostMyText account.
  5. In the window, click Open login in new tab.
  6. In the new tab, enter your HostMyText username and password.
  7. The tab shows “Linked! You can close this tab and return to HubSpot.”
  8. Close the tab.
  9. Close the window in HubSpot.

The card now shows your numbers.

Step C: Connect your numbers

Section titled “Step C: Connect your numbers”

Only a HostMyText organization admin can connect numbers.

The page shows all numbers of your organization.

You can connect only the numbers that you can use in HostMyText.

For other numbers, the page shows “No access to this number in HostMyText”.

Turn off auto-replies first

Section titled “Turn off auto-replies first”

The page shows this warning: “Turn off HostMyText auto-replies on a number before connecting it, or contacts may get two replies.”

Turn off the auto-replies on the number before you connect it.

Connect a number

Section titled “Connect a number”
  1. Open the app settings. See “Open the app settings” above.
  2. Find the section Phone numbers.
  3. In HubSpot inbox for new connections, check the inbox.
  4. Find your number in the table.
  5. Click Connect.
  6. The status changes to Connected.

HubSpot needs at least one conversations inbox.

If the account has no inbox, the page shows this message: “No inboxes were found in this HubSpot account, so numbers can’t be connected yet. Create a conversations inbox in HubSpot, then reload this page.”

Create an inbox in HubSpot.

Reload the page.

Step D: Choose the automation sender

Section titled “Step D: Choose the automation sender”

A workflow has no HubSpot user.

The app sends the workflow text as the automation sender.

The automation sender is one linked seat.

The app uses the HostMyText access of this user.

The sender can send only from the numbers that this user can use in HostMyText.

  1. Open the app settings.
  2. Find the section Automation sender.
  3. Choose a user in the list.
  4. Click Save.
  5. The page shows “Automation sender saved.”

If the list is empty, the page shows “No linked HubSpot seats yet”.

Link a seat first. See step B.

Warning about numbers

Section titled “Warning about numbers”

The page can show a warning like this: “This sender can’t send from: (numbers) — workflow sends using these numbers will fail until access is granted.”

Choose another sender, or give this user access to the numbers in HostMyText.

Remove the automation sender

Section titled “Remove the automation sender”

Choose No automation sender in the list.

Click Save.

The page shows “Automation sender cleared.”

Without an automation sender, workflow steps fail.

The step shows this message: “No automation sender is configured for this portal; pick one in the app’s settings.”

Step E: Each rep links a seat

Section titled “Step E: Each rep links a seat”

Each rep does step B one time.

Reps can use 06 Quick start.

Step F: Build a workflow that sends a text

Section titled “Step F: Build a workflow that sends a text”

This example sends a welcome text when a contact submits a form.

  1. Create a contact-based workflow in HubSpot.
  2. Set the trigger to a form submission.
  3. Add an action.
  4. Search for HostMyText. You can also look in the action list. Scroll to the category Integrated Apps and click HostMyText.
  5. Click Send HostMyText message.
  6. In Message, type your text. Example: Hi {{firstname}}, thank you for your request.
  7. In Send from, choose a number.
  8. Save the action.
  9. Turn on the workflow.

Message tokens

Section titled “Message tokens”

You can put these tokens in the message:

  • {{firstname}}
  • {{lastname}}
  • {{email}}
  • {{phone}}
  • {{mobilephone}}
  • {{company}}
  • {{jobtitle}}

The app replaces each token with the value from the contact.

You can also use the HubSpot property picker in the message field.

The two methods work at the same time.

Which number gets the text

Section titled “Which number gets the text”

The action sends the text to the mobile phone number of the contact.

If the contact has no mobile phone number, the action uses the phone number.

The list in “Send from”

Section titled “The list in “Send from””

The list shows the numbers that meet both rules:

  • The number is connected.
  • The automation sender can use the number.

Send later

Section titled “Send later”

To send later, add the HubSpot Delay step before the action.

When the step fails

Section titled “When the step fails”

The workflow continues after a failed step.

The step result shows the reason.

Result message Reason
Message sent. The step worked.
No sending number was configured on this action. The field Send from is empty.
This contact has no phone number on file. The contact has no mobile phone number and no phone number.
This contact has opted out of receiving messages and cannot be sent to. The contact sent STOP.
No automation sender is configured for this portal; pick one in the app’s settings. You did not choose an automation sender.
The automation sender does not have access to the From number. The sender cannot use this number in HostMyText.
The From number is not connected to HubSpot. The number in Send from is not connected. Connect it on the settings page, or choose a connected number. No text is sent.

For a failed step, HubSpot can show the message after a technical start that begins with “Unexpected status”. Read the text in the quotation marks. It is one of the messages in the table.

The text of a successful step appears in the HubSpot inbox thread of the contact.

The text also appears in HostMyText.
