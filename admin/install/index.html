Install and connect

Audience: HubSpot administrators and HostMyText organization administrators.

Read 01 Overview and requirements first.

Before you start

Section titled “Before you start”
  • Your HubSpot plan must be Sales Hub Professional or higher, or Service Hub Professional or higher.
  • You must be a HubSpot Super Admin, or a user with Marketplace access and the right permissions.
  • You must have your HostMyText admin username and password.
  • You must have the install link from HostMyText.

HostMyText sends you the install link by email.

Always use this link.

A different link can end with the message “HubSpot authentication failed”.

Steps

Section titled “Steps”

1. Open the install link

Section titled “1. Open the install link”

Open the link from the email in your browser.

Your browser goes to HubSpot.

2. Choose your HubSpot account

Section titled “2. Choose your HubSpot account”

If HubSpot asks, choose the HubSpot account to connect.

HubSpot checks your plan at this step.

If your plan is too low, HubSpot shows this message: “Couldn’t complete the connection. Authorization failed because your account lacks access to the required scopes.”

In this case, see 01 Overview and requirements for the plans that work.

3. Review the permissions

Section titled “3. Review the permissions”

HubSpot shows the consent screen.

The screen shows these items:

  • the HostMyText logo
  • the text “Connecting HostMyText to HubSpot”
  • the text “by hostmy.com”
  • your email address
  • a yellow box with this text: “This app hasn’t been reviewed or approved by HubSpot. Make sure you trust the developer before connecting it to your account.”
  • the list of permissions that the app needs

HubSpot has not reviewed the app yet.

This is why the yellow box appears.

The box is normal.

Read the list of permissions.

Click Connect app.

4. Confirm the unverified app dialog

Section titled “4. Confirm the unverified app dialog”

HubSpot shows a second dialog with the title “Connecting an unverified app”.

The dialog says that this app could put your data at risk.

It asks you to type “I accept the risk”.

Type the words in the field.

Continue.

HubSpot shows this dialog for every app that it has not reviewed.

5. Sign in to HostMyText

Section titled “5. Sign in to HostMyText”

HostMyText shows a sign-in page.

Enter your HostMyText admin username and password.

Only a HostMyText organization admin can connect a HubSpot account.

6. Check the result

Section titled “6. Check the result”

HostMyText shows the page “Your HubSpot account is connected”.

The page shows your HubSpot account number.

It shows this text: “HubSpot account (number) is now linked to your HostMyText organization.”

Click Return to HubSpot.

You can close the page after that.

What to do next

Section titled “What to do next”
  1. Open a contact in HubSpot.
  2. Link your seat from the HostMyText card.
  3. Open the Settings page of the app.
  4. Connect your numbers.
  5. Choose the automation sender.

Steps are in 03 Setup guide.

If something goes wrong

Section titled “If something goes wrong”
You see What it means What to do
Couldn’t complete the connection. Authorization failed because your account lacks access to the required scopes. Your HubSpot plan is too low. Use a Sales Hub or Service Hub Professional account or higher.
HubSpot authentication failed. The install did not start from the HostMyText link. Open the link from the HostMyText email again.
Your HubSpot connection session expired. Please try connecting again. The install took too long. Open the install link again.
The username or password you provided is incorrect. Please try again. The HostMyText sign-in failed. Enter your HostMyText username and password again.
Only an organization admin can connect a HubSpot portal. Your HostMyText user is not an organization admin. Sign in with an admin user.
This HubSpot portal is already connected to a different HostMyText organization. Contact HostMyText support to change it. The HubSpot account has a link to another organization. Contact HostMyText support: https://www.hostmy.com/get-support
This HostMyText organization is already connected to a different HubSpot portal. Contact HostMyText support to change it. The organization has a link to another HubSpot account. Contact HostMyText support: https://www.hostmy.com/get-support
Service is temporarily down. Please try again later. HostMyText had an internal problem. Try again later. If it continues, contact HostMyText support.

More help is in 05 Troubleshooting and FAQ.
